Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions across services, support, and mentorship courses

How do I reset my password or account access?

To reset your account access:

  1. Submit a support ticket below with your registered email address
  2. Select Account Issue as the issue type
  3. Our team verifies your identity and sends a secure reset link
  4. If you do not see our email, check your spam folder or message us on WhatsApp
What are your support hours?

Our support availability:

  • 24/7 Technical Support: Available for critical system issues
  • Phone Support: Monday-Friday, 9 AM - 6 PM IST (+91 8091401208)
  • Email Support: 24/7 at team.bunshailogicloop@gmail.com
  • Live Chat: 24/7 via our AI & support assistant
How do I submit and track a support ticket?

You can submit a support ticket right on this page using the form below. Upon submission you receive a unique reference ID (e.g. BST-XXXXXXXX) and an automated confirmation email. Include screenshots or error logs for faster resolution.

What is your average response time?

Our response times vary based on priority:

  • Critical Issues: Within 15 minutes (24/7)
  • High Priority: Within 1 hour
  • Medium Priority: Within 2 hours
  • Low Priority / General: Within 4–24 hours
How do I download the detailed syllabus for a mentorship course?

Visit our Courses & Mentorship Programs page or any specific program page, click View & Download Syllabus, and fill in your name, email, and phone number. Your multi-page PDF syllabus downloads immediately.

Are mentorship classes live, and do you provide placement support?

Yes! All 14 mentorship programs across Digital Marketing, Data Science & Analytics, AI Programs, Full Stack with AI Engineering, Cyber Security, and Cloud Computing run in live mentor-led cohorts (4–8 months) with real capstone projects, resume reviews, mock interviews, and placement assistance.

Do you provide phone and emergency incident support?

Yes—call our 24/7 helpline at +91 8091401208 or reach us on WhatsApp at the same number for urgent production outages or security incidents.
