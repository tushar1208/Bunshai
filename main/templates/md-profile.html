Professional Biography

With 4+ years of hands-on experience across software, cloud and technology management, our Managing Director combines an engineering foundation (B.Tech) with business strategy (MBA). His journey began as a developer, where he saw first-hand how the right technology can transform a business - and how the right training can transform a career.

In 2025 he founded BunShai TECHNOHUB to deliver affordable, high-quality technology services and career-focused mentorship programs. Today he leads a team of directors and engineers, personally guides strategy, client relationships and the mentorship curriculum.

A Day in the Life

A typical working day - from first coffee to the last follow-up.

6:00 AM

Wake-up, Workout & Planning

An hour of exercise followed by a quiet 20 minutes writing down the three outcomes that must happen today.

8:00 AM

Inbox, Dashboards & Lead Review

Checks new enquiries from the website and chatbot, support tickets, course enrollments and the overnight project status.

9:30 AM

Leadership Stand-up

A 20-minute sync with the Technical Director, Creative Director and Development Team Head - blockers, priorities and deadlines.

11:00 AM

Client Discovery & Proposal Calls

Meets prospective clients to understand their problem, scope the solution and approve customised proposals.

1:00 PM

Lunch & Student Doubt Session

Joins the mentorship cohorts for open Q&A - career guidance, project feedback and resume reviews.

2:30 PM

Deep Work - Strategy & Partnerships

Focused, no-meeting block for budgets, partnerships, hiring plans and the 2027 roadmap.

4:30 PM

Product & Design Review

Reviews builds with the Development Head and designs with the Creative Director before anything ships to a client.

6:30 PM

Team Check-out & Customer Follow-ups

Closes the loop on tickets and enquiries, and replies to the founders' mailbox personally.

8:30 PM

Learning Hour

Reads, practises for the next certification or prepares talks - because a leader who stops learning stops leading.
