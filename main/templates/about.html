Our Story

From vision to reality - our journey from 2025 to 2027

2025

The Beginning

BunShai TECHNOHUB was founded in 2025 with a vision to provide affordable, high-quality technology solutions and career-ready skill programs. We started with a small team of passionate engineers, designers and mentors, and delivered our first client projects within months.

2026

Growth & Mentorship Programs

We expanded our services across IT consulting, software development, cloud, cybersecurity, data analytics and digital marketing, and launched our mentorship programs - 14 programs across 6 career tracks including AI, Full Stack, Cyber Security and Cloud Computing - while starting to serve clients beyond India.

2027

Vision Ahead - Scaling with AI

Our roadmap for 2027: scale AI-driven products and automation, grow our mentorship cohorts with placement-focused tracks, deepen enterprise partnerships and build a stronger global presence while keeping quality and affordability at our core.
